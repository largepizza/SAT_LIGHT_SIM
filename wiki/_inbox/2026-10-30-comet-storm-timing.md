## Now
The Great Comet's meteor storm happens when the Earth passes through the comet's dust, which is only round its closest
approach to the comet (2037-03-17 03:30 UTC, 560,000 km). The meteoroids are big grains shed round the sungrazing perihelion;
flown from the comet's orbit they are still a compact cloud a few million km round the nucleus a month later, and the Earth
is within 3 million km of any of it only from 03-16 ~06:00 to 03-17 ~19:00 (tens of millions of km before: 23 million km from
the comet's orbit on 03-08). ZHR: 8000 x 10^(-5 |d|) before the peak (half-maximum 1.4 h before), 10^(-4 d) after, over a
residual of 400 rising with the spike and decaying with an e-fold of 75 d. Total 8400 at the peak, ~1870 three hours before,
ZHR 10 at 2037-03-16 13:27 (the event's start, 14 h before), ~265 on 04-17, under 10 after 2037-12-22. Radiant and speed unchanged.

## History
Until 2026-10-30 (issue 20261030_141206) the storm started 9 days before the closest approach (ZHR 10 on 03-08, spike slopes
0.9 / 0.45 per day, the residual rising with an e-fold of 2.5 d), while the Earth was still ~23 million km from the dust; at
00:21 on 03-17 it read 6500. The 75-day decay is artistic (physically the Earth leaves the cloud at ~1.2 million km a day).
