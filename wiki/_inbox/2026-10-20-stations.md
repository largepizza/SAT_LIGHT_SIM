## Now
**Station models come from a generator.** `python tools/make_station_models.py` writes Haven-1, Haven-2, Axiom,
Orbital Reef, ROS, BAS and Starlab, and appends render-only `gb_*` detail to the hand-made ISS and Tiangong models
(re-running replaces it). Layouts follow each operator's concept art and published dimensions, cited in each
model's `sources`:
- Haven-1: a 10.1 x 4.4 m hull with a conical nose, two wings on yokes at the aft end, a docked Dragon.
- Haven-2: a 7 m core with four 16 m modules docked radially in a cross, a pair of wings on each module, and
  radiator fins on the core.
- Axiom: 11 m modules with a curved array over the hull's zenith half. The power module carries ISS-style
  Kapton wings.
- Orbital Reef: a 7 m core with two LIFE inflatables, an aft truss of large wings, and forward radiators.
- ROS: the December 2025 plan (Nauka, UUM, NEM, ShM, a base module and two target modules).
- BAS: five 3.8 m modules.
- Starlab: an 8 m domed habitat with four wings in a 45° cross.

Every wing turns about its own long axis and is attached by a yoke or mast. Photometric hulls are split into one
profile per meridian segment, so each piece's occluder (a frustum through its end radii) matches the shape. Detail
is render-only: ports, dishes, windows, bands, arms and visiting spacecraft.

**Magnitudes moved:** mean m1000 for Haven-2 went 1.41 -> 0.78, Orbital Reef 1.53 -> 0.34, ROS 2.25 -> 1.44 and
BAS 2.24 -> 1.52. Starlab went fainter, 1.68 -> 2.39. Axiom and Haven-1 changed little. ISS and Tiangong are
unchanged.
