## Now
The roster ends with the **GEO belt**: 398 real geostationary satellites (Intelsat, SES, Eutelsat, Viasat/Inmarsat,
EchoStar/DirecTV, Telesat, Chinese, Russian, Indian, Japanese and other fleets, GOES / Meteosat / Himawari /
Fengyun / Elektro / INSAT weather satellites, TDRS and some military), each by name at its slot, from
`data/geo_satellites.json` (written by `tools/make_geo_belt.py`; slots are mid-2020s positions rounded to 0.1 deg,
approximate for 2036). It is a new orbit distribution, `"distribution": "List"`: named `members` (inline or
`"members_file"`), each with its own type (`type`, or `class` through `class_types`); a member with `lon_deg` is
geostationary and stays over its slot (drift < 0.01 deg a year; inclination 0.02-0.08 deg unless `incl_deg`).
Four generic models stand in for the classes (`geo_comsat_large`, `geo_comsat_medium`, `geo_weather`,
`geo_spinner`, from `tools/make_geo_models.py`): box bus, N/S OSR radiators, two Sun-tracking wings, E/W
reflectors; one-wing weather satellite; MSG-style drum. Typical brightness 11-16 mag. The selection panel and
info window show the member's own name, operator, mission and slot; a one-satellite entry (a station) shows its
name without "#0".
Settings > Constellations is grouped by `"category"` (Broadband, Stations and depots, Orbital data centers,
Reflectors, Geostationary belt, Science, Benchmark shells, Debris), groups folded at first, each with ON / HLT for
the whole group; stations have Select / Go to; a List entry has LIST: searchable, paged members with Select / Go to.
Show / Highlight are per-constellation bits in the orbit pass's type-buffer header (256 entries).

## History
Issue 20261020_190331 asked for real GEO satellites and a Constellations tab that does not list stations as
constellations of one. The Show / Highlight masks were two 32-bit push-constant words: any entry past the 32nd
(and Kessler / launch records, whose constellation index was masked to 5 bits) silently read another entry's
bit. The comsat models bake to 112 exact lobes; at that count the ~200 GEO satellites above any horizon cost
~0.3 ms of orbit dispatch from the ground, so they bake at the 48-lobe budget (validator p95 < 0.01 mag); A/B
GEO on vs off in one app: orbit dispatch +0.05-0.07 ms from orbit, GPU frame within noise.
