## Now
**Screenshots and issue shots can keep the UI.** Settings > Display > SCREENSHOTS: "Screenshots (F12) include the UI"
(`display.screenshot_ui`, default off) and "Issue report shots include the UI" (`display.report_shot_ui`, default
off; also a toggle at the top of the Issue reports window). A new key, "Screenshot with UI" (default F10, rebindable),
always keeps it. A shot with the UI leaves out what exists only because of the shot: its own toast, the issue note
bar and the Issue reports window (`shotHidesOverlays`). Every other open window (a trace, the info window) is in it.
The shot is still the frame of the key press (no settle). Harness `capture ui=1` is unaffected; `ui key` now takes
f7 / f8 / f9 / f10 / f12.

**The info window leads with what a player wants.** Default height fills the right side down to the HUD panel
(min 560). Under the render and the four tiles: ACTIONS, always visible — Trace pass, Go to (Leave while following),
Track, then Explode and ASAT strike as orange danger pills that take two clicks (the first arms for 3 s; the result
or the reason it was refused is a toast). The scrolling sections, in order: STATUS (object, constellation, satellite
type, operational / dead / launch / debris; for a rocket or re-entry: the stage and burn, T+, altitude, speed),
BRIGHTNESS, SKY POSITION, ORBIT (not for rockets / re-entries) open; MODEL (TECHNICAL) (type, model id, size,
triangles and parts), 3D VIEW (camera, light, pose, spin, shadows, reflections, detail, glare, markers, reset) and
PHOTOMETRY CHECK collapsed. The separate BREAKUP section is gone.

**The legacy-lighting Gen2 comparison shell is gone** from the shipped roster (`data/constellations.json`, both the
disabled constellation and its two-surface type). A legacy Gen2 is still in
`data/custom/constellations_attitude_example.json`.
