## Now
The intro is an opening the player plays: its first frame sets 2036-11-25 23:15:46.57 UTC, the intro cloud phase
(2.356), the observer at 33.564 N, 91.289 W on the ground (Mississippi delta) facing north up Starmind's train
(az 3.19, el 18.25, FOV 46.5), FPS mode on, the clock 1x running; from then on nothing drives the camera or the
clock. The seven captions (one per songbeat, 7.61 s; "SAT LIGHT SIM" at 45.66 s; the end at 53.27 s) play on a
frame-time clock with the music; the band never takes input and sits 90 px up, or clear of the corner toolbars when
they show (`uiBottomClearance`). The corner toolbars and their popups are held back until the player is first in
menu mode (Tab, right click, any forced exit) or the captions end. No skip: Space is Pause, pad Start keeps its
action; "Play intro on startup" (`display.play_intro_on_startup`) is the off switch; "Replay Intro" closes Settings
and replays from the same moment and place. The UC1 graphics benchmark averages GPU frame time over the 53.27-s
caption period (12 ms target; > 18 ms steps down, < 4.8 ms steps up; unchanged rule). Measured 2026-11-16 on the
dev machine (1600x900, the player panning): Medium 17.4 ms (stays), High 21.3 ms (stays: High is never moved), Low 14.5 ms
(stays), Planetarium 3.6 ms (stays: the rule has no step up from Planetarium). Harness: `intro play [at=] [bench=1]`, `intro skip` (ends the captions), `intro state` (`caption_index`,
`toolbars_shown`, `fps_mode`, `observer`, `camera`, `bench`), `inertial move ... boost=1`, `ui key space`;
`tools/harness/scripts/opening.satcmd`, `opening_sky_bench.satcmd`; `ui_lowres.py` view `intro_caption_menu`.

## History
Until 2026-11-16 the intro flew the cinematic intro2's eight keys to orbit (cineEval spline, arrival remap at the
title, FOV widened to 105 deg), drove sim time at 5 s per beat with the clock paused, locked the HUD and input
(a full-screen capture rect) and could be skipped with Space or pad Start. Supersedes the "Now" of
2026-11-13-intro2.md and 2026-11-14-introhold.md (their dates and moment still hold; the flight does not).
