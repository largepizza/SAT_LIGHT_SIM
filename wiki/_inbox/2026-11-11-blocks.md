## Now
- The cyclone flow map's displacement (the turning texture around a hurricane and a supercell's mesocyclone) is
  evaluated at every main step of the cloud march on a ray near a vortex; the light march's samples of that step reuse
  it. The occupancy clipmap's bake evaluates it at each of its points, so the march and the bake read the same field and
  the clipmap stays conservative inside a hurricane.
- Cost: none measurable away from vortices; at a hurricane view the march is faster (cloud march 28.1 -> 23.4 ms at
  issue_20261111_165715 shot 1, RTX 3070 Ti, 1920x1009), because the clipmap now skips what the march does not draw.
- Known issue (open): from about 1 km above the ground close to a tornado (inside or near its debris and wall-cloud
  plume), the plume composite's front / behind split against the cloud's opacity profile draws dark rectangles along
  the horizon and a rectangular notch in the funnel (issues 20261111_171327, 20261111_171428). Not the clipmap, not the
  flow map.

## History
- Until 2026-11-11 the displacement was held for 1 km of the ray ("it varies over ~10 km"). The differential turn
  shears it at kilometres per kilometre near the radius of maximum wind, so the march drew another place's clouds
  (stippled, smeared walls) and the clipmap, baked with the true displacement, skipped them at its cells' faces:
  rectangular blocks in hurricane walls. The clipmap's miss counter read 1.47M of 12.9M skipped steps against the held
  value and 53 against the point's own.
