## Now
The occupancy clipmap's bake reads each cell at a 3 x 3 x 4 pattern (heights 160 m apart) and then, for every column,
again at 30 / 80 / 130 m above the low field's own base (`gCv2BaseLow` from the pattern's reads), where those heights
fall inside the pattern's range: a low cloud thinner than the pattern's height spacing (the margin of a flat-based
cumulus, a field of thin humilis) lies on its base and is caught there. Setting `clouds_v2.cdc_base_band` (default 1,
0 = off; no UI; part of the clipmap epoch). At issue_20261110_182715 shot 1 the steps the clipmap skipped that held
cloud went from 46.8k (0.9%) to 27.
The view march's coarse strides (1.5-2.5 steps) draw their share from a hash of the ray's jitter and the step index
(`cv2StrideU`), so over the jitters a pixel visits, the coarse samples land uniformly in depth: a cloud thinner than
the stride converges to its mean instead of to rings of equal distance round the nadir.

## History
Until 2026-11-10 the bake read only the 36-point pattern and the march's coarse strides were fract(jitter + 0.618 i).
Thin low clouds were cut along the clipmap's cells (stair-stepped edges, isolated square puffs: "Pixelated cloud
edges", issue_20261110_182715, with any settings), and the golden sequence, every stride a function of one number,
left a depth-periodic bias (hit share 0.20 +- 0.074 in a model of the walk; hashed +- 0.001): concentric rings round
the nadir at the stride's period (the "wood grain" contour rings of 2026-10-13 were the same bias, half-fixed by the
random-walk strides). Ring ripple at the report's shot 4, ring sector, band amplitude 0.88 -> 0.21 levels. The hashed
strides converge a little noisier (Monte Carlo instead of the golden sequence's low discrepancy).
