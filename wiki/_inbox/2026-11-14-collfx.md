## Now
A collision seen up close gets its flash, fireball, sound and held satellites whether the projectile is a tracked
object (a pair collision) or one of the small 0.1-1 m fragments of the density grid (a grid hit). A grid hit has no
projectile to draw on the approach; its flash and sound come from the fragment's mass and the hit's speed. Grid
hits are ~10-20x the pair collisions (40 against 2 within 3000 km of an orbiting eye in an hour, day 72 of the
Sun's Fury), mostly tens-of-gram pieces cratering debris, so one is drawn only when its peak flash at the eye is
magnitude 1 or brighter: about as many as the pair collisions (5 against 2 in that hour).
