## Now
The cloud march attenuates each lit sample's light by the air between the eye and that sample (`cv2AirOD`: Rayleigh
+ aerosol column on the straight line between the eye's and the sample's heights), and hands the sky pass an air
split distance where the air transmittance equals the samples' light-weighted mean (not their mean distance); the
exact path's attenuation at the split corrects the estimate. A ray that meets rain a few hundred metres out and cloud
tens of km out (the eye in or beside a rain curtain, looking at the horizon) thus gives the far cloud its haze and the
rain almost none. The resolve still averages the split over the 3x3 new samples, so a pattern of the far cloud's
shapes along the horizon remains, weaker (band std 9.1 -> 7.5 levels at the reporter's view).
