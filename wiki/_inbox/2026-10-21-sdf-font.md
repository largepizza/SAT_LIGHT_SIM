## Now
The UI font is a signed distance field: ASCII 32-126 baked once at startup by stb_truetype's `stbtt_GetCodepointSDF`
at 48 px with 8 texels of padding into a 1024x1024 R8 atlas (value 128 on the outline, 16 per texel of distance).
The UI shader turns the distance into coverage over one screen pixel (texels per pixel from the UV derivatives), with
a slight emboldening (up to 0.1 px) only under 13 px. Text is crisp at any size, about 9 px to the intro's 100 px
titles; metrics (advances, glyph origins) are the same stb_truetype values as before, so no layout moved, and at 1080p
the ink matches the old bitmap within ~1%. Because small text no longer breaks up, "Scale with window" may now take
text down to 12 px (was 16 px) on a short window (`fs()`, `kAutoFontFloorPx`), and the loading screen scales down to
0.8 on a short window (was never below 1). Harness: `ui hover <ElementId>[:i]` / `ui hover off` rests the scripted
pointer over an element (tooltips in a capture).

## History
Until 2026-10-21 the font was a plain `stbtt_BakeFontBitmap` bitmap (32, then 48 px) scaled per size by a ratio:
blocky when upscaled (intro captions; a serif face was dropped in 2026-09 for the same reason) and, minified without
mips, its thin strokes broke up under ~16 px — hence the 16-px floor that kept the UI from shrinking at 720p / 768p.
`tools/harness/ui_lowres.py run` passes all 160 views with the 12-px floor.
