## Now
- A launch's exhaust parcels are built on a worker thread (at most 4 builds in flight). A finished build that its launch has
  not asked for in 4 frames (the launch left view, the clock jumped) is dropped and rebuilt when the launch is drawn again.

## History
- Until 2026-11-11 such builds kept their slots for the rest of the session; after four of them no plume was ever built
  again and rockets flew without exhaust (issue_20261111_211610 "No plumes?": six launch jumps then the report's view
  reproduced it, 0 parcels; 500 after the fix).
