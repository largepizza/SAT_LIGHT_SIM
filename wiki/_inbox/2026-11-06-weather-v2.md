## Now
Weather v2. One analytic wind (zonal climatology, Rossby waves, mesoscale curl) moves everything; the cloud map no
longer drifts as a whole. The weather map is synthesised: a climatology (ITCZ, dry subtropics, storm tracks, cold-sea
decks), the weather systems and moisture noise advected by the wind per octave, so cloud fields evolve at real rates
(lifetime about linear in size: ~1 h at 64 km, ~4.5 h at 512 km, ~1 day at 2000 km, from NOAA GMGSI hourly IR).

Storms are a convection cube baked with the map: each storm cell's intensity and column top (its column as an exact
distance field at 5 km), the anvil as the integral of what the storm detrained at the tropopause since its tower
reached it (spreading ~7 m/s, streaming downwind, decaying over its linger), and the mid-level outflow (trailing
stratiform). The cumulus field draws all of it: a storm is the cumulus layer pushed up into a column that fans out
under the lid. Squall lines trail their shield rearward and lift a shelf cloud on the gust front ahead (a low wedge,
flat-based, rising to the towers' bases, rain-free, clear air ahead). Tropical cyclones are the same fields (eyewall
ring, log-spiral bands, the CDO as the cube's mass) around a stadium eye that clears every layer.

Every reader of the map (cloud layers, far field, clipmap, radar, lightning, eye rain) reads it in one frame; the
flow warp that bends bands is baked into the background only. Haboobs are retired.

## History
Until 2026-11-06 the map was the real satellite image, drifting as one sheet (~16 deg of longitude per 12 h) and
evolving with 1500-km growth waves that decorrelated the large scales within hours. Storms were props walked per sample:
towers, capsule anvil shields, stamps in the bake, a CDO swirl texture; their cloud field stood ~100 km from their
columns because the march read the map through the flow warp while the storms were placed without it. Haboobs drew
gust-front dust walls (2026-10-23 to 2026-11-06).
