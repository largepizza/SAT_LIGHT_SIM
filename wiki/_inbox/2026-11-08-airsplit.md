## Now
The sky pass splits the air in front of a pixel's clouds at one distance. Where wildfire smoke (or any plume that
carries the air split) lies past a cloud's first contact, the composite gives that smoke its own distance: `dustCloudsCS`
returns the plumes' light-weighted distance past the contact (`tBack`), the segment loop their visible opacity in
front of / past it, and the two merge in air-brightness space with the smoke weighted as all the composite's opacity
but the cloud's. The same holds with no opacity profile on the frame's ray when the resolved cloud is nearer than the
smoke. The merged distance travels to the full-resolution combine in DA.a, flagged by the half float's lowest mantissa
bit. Air brightness (`smokeAirSplitM`) is 1 - e^-tau with tau the ray's own optical depth: 0.108 per molecular column
+ 0.0138 per aerosol column (t / 40 km for a level sea-level ray, about half the old rate above the haze); the function
accepts the smoke beyond the cloud.

## History
2026-11-08, issues 20261108_171841 ("Black line on horizon", 14 km up) and 20261108_172203 ("Render artifacts within
rain", inside a rain curtain): both were a wildfire pall 200-300 km out behind a nearer cloud (a high veil; rain a
few hundred m out) taking that cloud's air split: a dark line, and half-res grey blocks. Before, smoke behind a cloud
was skipped by the split (only smoke in front of the cloud pulled it). Measured at the reports' views: line dip
26-42 -> <= 4 levels; horizon band std 8.7 -> 2.8 (2.7 with no wildfire at all).
