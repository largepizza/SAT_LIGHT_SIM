## Now
The intro is a saved Cinematics-window path: 8 keys, one per songbeat (7.61 s, 53.3 s total). It opens in the
Mississippi delta (33.56 N, 91.29 W) at twilight under Starmind's trains and a Reflect beam on the eastern horizon.
It then climbs past a Starship launched from Starbase, Texas over the Gulf, and ends in orbit (638 km) with the Sun
setting on the limb. It always plays at 2036-11-25 23:15:46.57 UTC, 330.6 s after that launch lifts off. The Moon
is 50% lit (first quarter), and the cloud-map phase is 2.356 rad (the drift rate is not changed). The intro drives
sim time itself at 5 s per beat (0.657x), so each key shows the sky it was framed on. At the end it hands the clock
back running at 1x. The path is `cineEval`'s spline: log altitude, log FOV and unwrapped azimuth, with the first
and last segments eased. Harness command: `intro [state|play|skip]`.

## History
The first intro (California coast, Topaz) used the boot time 2036-11-22 02:06 UTC and only moved altitude, az, el
and FOV. That boot time is unchanged; the harness uses it as its default moment. The new cinematic was framed on
2036-11-21 under a 16% crescent. The user asked for a quarter-to-half Moon, for some ambient light at night.
`launch list` over Nov 23-27 found the same Starbase-Texas twilight launch geometry on Nov 25 (Moon 50%).
