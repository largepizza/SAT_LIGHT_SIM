## Now
Ahead of a haboob's gust front the low cloud and rain are cleared outright (1 - smoothstep(0, 0.8, z) of the dust grid's
clear-air value), so the dust wall stands under open sky with the storm behind it. The cloud march is built twice
(specialization constant 3, `kCv2DustOn`): the host binds the version without the haboob code whenever the dust grid holds
no wall (`dustGpuOn`), which saves 0.5-1.2 ms of cloud march in every view without a haboob. The wildfire smoke's Sun
transmittance in the sky pass is skipped under full cloud shadow.

## History
Perf pass 4 (2026-10-25): the clear air multiplied the cloud by 0.05, leaving a thin veil every ray marched and lit (7 ms
at the haboob Watch with the user's settings); and the haboob code, merely present in the inlined field, cost every view
0.5-1.2 ms (code size: a uniform gate did not help). Matrix (21 cells, summed GPU): 492.5 ms before the 2026-10-24 reworks,
501.6 after them, 494.2 after this pass.
