## Now
The Sun's disc is drawn at its true angular radius (`sunAngRTrue`, 0.262-0.271 deg over the year) times
"Sun size (x real)" (Settings > Sky > Refraction and mirages; key `clouds.sun_size`, range 0.25-4, default 1, like
"Moon size"). The CPU passes the drawn radius in the frame UBO (`surgeInfo.w`, rad); the full sky, the
Planetarium / probe variants (SKY_LITE / SKY_ENV) and Potato (`sat_sky_minimal.frag`) all draw the same radius, and the
refraction table's visible-disc share integrates over it. Within ~2 deg of the Moon the disc still eases to its true
size whatever the slider, so eclipses look the same at any setting. The harness `state` reports `sun.size_x` and
`sun.drawn_radius_deg`.

## History
Until 2026-11-18 (issue 20261118_182727, "make the sun size 1x be the default, just like the moon") the disc was a fixed
0.007 rad (~1.5x true; "Sun size" 1.5 reproduces it) and Potato's ~2.6x (a cos smoothstep 0.99988-0.99997).
