## Now
- "Hurricane eye cloud" (`clouds_v2.hurricane_eye_cloud`, default 1, range 0-1, Clouds tab slot 608; UBO `hurrlook.x`):
  how much of the ~600 m low floor deck inside a hurricane's eye radius stays. 1 = all (the overcast eye with a few
  holes), 0.5 = the half over the weaker cells, 0 = a clear eye. Taken away cell by cell (the cell + cluster noise the
  eye's edge wanders by) in `cv2LowA`, after the eye's coverage cut; outside the eye radius (the stadium wall) nothing
  changes. 165853 shot 1 (45 km over the eye, 1600x900): mean luminance in the eye 175 / 156 / 91, cloud share
  0.999 / 0.886 / 0.195 at 1 / 0.5 / 0.
- The cyclone's cirrus deck (`cv2CycSpiral` mode 1) draws the arms' cirrus and the OUTFLOW CANOPY: the outflow sheet
  ("Hurricane outflow cirrus", to its reach) thinned by two whole-count waves of streaks along an anticyclonic spiral
  ~60 deg off the tangent (theta - 0.55 ln r), each bent by a slow angular wave, "Hurricane outflow streaks" their
  contrast (stronger toward the edge); the edge notched inward by two angular waves, the streaks reaching furthest. It
  never reaches past 1.3 of the outflow's reach (the anvil sampler's early out holds).
- "Hurricane minor swirls" now shapes only the low cloud (the weather bake); the deck no longer draws them.
