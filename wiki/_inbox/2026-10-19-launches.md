## Now
Constellations are kept flying by launches. Each orbital plane of a constellation that a program in `launches.json`
matches is refreshed once per design life (5-7 years, x "Satellite design life"): a rocket lifts off from a site the
plane passes over (sites, vehicles and programs in `launches.json`; Starship, Falcon 9, New Glenn, Vulcan, Ariane 6,
Long March, LVM3), deploys a train of satellites low (a string 120 m apart), the train climbs over weeks and arrives
exactly on its slots, and the satellites it replaces deorbit and re-enter as fireballs. The number of satellites never
changes. At the shipped roster that is ~13 Starship launches a day (Starmind), ~15k satellites climbing and ~18k
coming down at any moment. Rockets are bright points (a flame's magnitude per stage), boosters fly back to the pad or
a droneship position with boostback, entry and landing burns, expendable upper stages re-enter. The exhaust is drawn
as a physically modelled plume: a trail near the ground that the wind carries, and high up a bell expanding for tens
of km that is sunlit at twilight while the ground is dark (the "jellyfish"). Upper stages may vent their remaining
propellant while spinning, which seen from below makes a dump spiral like the 2009 Norway one; Starship's ship
re-enters whole as an 8-minute plasma glow. The roar arrives at the speed of sound.
All of it is a pure function of sim time. Settings > Constellations > LAUNCHES: on/off (`launches.enabled`), design
life (`launches.life_scale` 1), exhaust plumes (`launches.plume_gain` 1), Watch the next launch / a twilight launch /
a retired satellite re-entering. During the Sun's Fury there are no launches for three weeks after the storm, and a
satellite the storm or the cascade killed is not replaced. Default bookmark "Twilight Launch from Vandenberg".

## History
Built 2026-10-18/19 on the kessler branch (plan .plans/LAUNCHES_PLAN.md). The plume needed a multiple-scattering term
(daytime trails were dark grey) and a night boost (the night exposure is not physical); launches got pad
deconfliction after two Starships left Starbase 7 minutes apart. Measured cost with a launch in view: +0.3 ms GPU,
0.3 ms CPU.
