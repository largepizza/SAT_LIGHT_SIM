## Now
Deep convection (a storm mass, a congestus-typed region, a storm object's towers) carries the cumulus-scale lobes
(~0.2-0.8 km at the default 3.1 km shape period) on its storm-scale lobes (~0.8-6 km) at the weight "Storm billow lobes"
(`clouds_v2.storm_billow_lobes`, default 2.0, Settings > Weather; GPU `storms2.x`), blended in by the deep-convection share
from "Storm cumulus fine lobes" (`cu.w`), which every other cloud keeps, and faded back to it 25-100 km away (footprints 25-100 m), where those lobes are a few
pixels. The towers read as stacked cauliflower heads.
Cloud march cost at the issue's view 14.24 -> 14.33 ms (RTX 3070 Ti, 1920x1009; A-A 14.22), 128 registers.

## History
Until 2026-11-12 deep convection used "Storm cumulus fine lobes" (0.28 at the user's settings) for those lobes. At
"Storm feature size" 30 (clamped to 16) x the 3.1 km period the storm-scale lobes are kilometres wide, so storm towers
were smooth peaked ridges with only the erosion's fine grain (issue_20261112_220642 "Storm shape").
