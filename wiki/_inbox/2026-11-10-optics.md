## Now
The ice halos (cirrus, snow shafts, diamond dust, the rain / snow particles at the eye) are predicted from crystal
geometry: `cv2IceOptics` reads `assets/textures/ice_optics.rgba8`, a 244 x 277 x 31 RGBA8 table (8.4 MB) that
`tools/make_ice_optics.py` ray-traces through hexagonal ice prisms (Monte-Carlo geometric optics, Fresnel + total
internal reflection, per colour channel) for five crystal populations: random columns (22 deg halo), compact random
crystals (46 deg halo), plates falling flat (sundogs, parhelic circle, circumzenithal arc below 32.2 deg of Sun,
circumhorizon arc above 57.8, subsun, subparhelia), singly oriented columns (tangent arcs, the circumscribed halo
above ~29 deg, supralateral / infralateral arcs) and Parry-oriented columns (Parry arcs). The table is indexed by the
scattering angle from the light, the azimuth about it and the light's elevation over the crystals' own vertical
(from orbit the ice sample's, not the eye's; a light below it gives the mirror pattern), and weighted by the region's
habit (`cv2IceHabit`): x random, y plates, z well-aligned, w compact, with the shares "Ice optics: column share"
(`clouds_v2.ice_optics_columns`, 0.5) and "Ice optics: Parry share" (`ice_optics_parry`, 0.2). "Ice optics: traced
crystal table" (`ice_optics_table`, 1) switches back to the analytic lobes, which also stand in when the asset is
missing. The traced 22 deg halo peak is scaled to the approved analytic level (`CV2_IO_GAIN` 0.25); the relative
brightness of every other feature is the trace's. The sun pillar stays analytic (wobbling plates). The tool's
`--check` compares the traced positions with the closed forms (minimum deviations 21.9 / 45.9 deg at 550 nm, the
sundog's distance vs elevation, the CZA / CHA cutoffs) within one 0.5 deg texel.

## History
Until 2026-11-10 the halos were analytic lobes placed at Snell's-law positions (22 / 46 deg rings, sundogs, parhelic
circle, CZA) with hand-set strengths; nothing was predicted beyond those five, the lobes used the eye's vertical
from orbit, and tangent, Parry, supralateral, circumhorizon arcs, the subsun and subparhelia did not exist.
