## Now
The sky pass integrates the atmosphere's densities over each step (the ring fix) at about the cost of the old midpoint
samples: one reciprocal for the three columns, the god rays' weights computed once per ray, and one loop for the god-ray
reads of a step and of the piece in front of the clouds. The terrain's sun-shadow march is skipped where the clouds'
shadow already takes the direct sun. The clouds' temporal resolve takes its neighbourhood boxes once per 2x2 block on the
sparse grid (shared memory): 1.5 -> 1.1 ms at 1920x1009. The cloud density clipmap checks and bakes only the levels the
march reads (cdc_max_level, 6): its pass went from 0.55-1.3 ms to ~0.1 ms at every preset. "God ray reads (near, 8 =
full)" (Weather tab, Rebuild experiments; `clouds_v2.godray_reads`, default 8) is an opt-in: 4 saves 0.5-1.1 ms of the sky
pass. Harness `shaders reload sky=<spv> sky_taa=<spv>` reloads the full sky pass from other SPVs for interleaved A/Bs.

## History
The 2026-10-24 merges cost the sky pass +0.5..+0.9 ms in every matrix cell (the ring fix: three divisions and an extra exp
a step, four exps a step for the god-ray weights, a second inlined copy of the froxel lookups); perf pass 3 recovered it
without changing the image. The cloud march's apparent +0.3..+0.6 ms near the ground was noise (interleaved: within
+-0.4). Tried and dropped: caching the froxel depth slices along a ray (slower), skipping froxels above the Sun volume (0),
dead-code removal (binary -34 KB, no gain), gating the ground's shading on the cloud composite (0).
