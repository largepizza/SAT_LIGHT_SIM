## Now
The moonlit sky, the Moon's corona and its wide halo are display-space terms added after the tonemap. They are weighted
by what the pixel shows of the air: behind a cloud by the cloud's transmittance, in front of it by the share of the
ray's Rayleigh in-scatter (1 - e^-tau) that lies before the cloud (the composite's air split, the front column by the
midpoint rule). A cloud 15 km off keeps ~10-25% of the glow, one on the horizon all of it. The halo stays cut at the
horizon (hClip) for both shares, as the open sky and sea beside the cloud are. SKY_LITE / SKY_ENV are unchanged.
## History
Until 2026-11-14 these terms ignored clouds: from 5.8 km at night a near storm cloud wore the whole sky's halo above
the horizon and none below it, a sharp line across its face at the horizon's height (+10.8 levels; after 1.7).
