## Now
The cloud texture round a tropical cyclone turns about its centre with the circulation and rides with the storm (the
cyclone flow map, `cv2CycFlowAt`): a modified Rankine vortex, vmax / rmw x "Hurricane rotation" (default 0.6) inside the
radius of maximum wind and x (r / rmw)^-1.5 outside, fading to nothing over the outer half of the bands' reach,
counter-clockwise in the north. The weather cube's stamp (the spiral bands, the deck, the eye) is the storm's envelope and
stays quasi-stationary in its frame; the cloud elements stream through it. Every noise read of every layer takes the
displacement, folded once per sample into the sample position, so the march stays at 128 registers. A rotation by rate x
time would wind up without bound, so it is a two-phase flow map like the cirrus fibres': each phase is a rotation by the
rate x its own age from a jittered start, renewed in turn every "Hurricane flow winding" (0.25 turn at the RMW: a window
of 5 min .. 2 h, its clock integrated per cyclone on the CPU), staggered over the disc; within "Hurricane flow blend"
(0.12 of the window each side of a renewal) the two phases are dithered per ray and the history averages them. The
storm lock drags the noise with the centre (the storm's velocity through the drifting noise frame, east / north at the
centre, as far as the reach's fade band keeps its shear under ~0.5), so a storm's clouds no longer slide through it with
the global drift. The resolve reprojects the turn and the lock for the cyclone the eye is deepest in. The CDO sheet's
ripple turns with the content, and its lumps scale with the CDO's radius, come in two sizes and raise and lower the top
by up to 200 m so the turn shows from above. The bake reads the arms' ragged texture in the storm's frame (it travels
with the storm), and the automatic arm count follows the storm's peak wind, so it is constant over its life.
Settings: `clouds_v2.hurricane_rotation` 0.6, `hurricane_flow_turns` 0.25, `hurricane_flow_blend` 0.12 (Clouds tab,
slots 560-562). The cyclone record on the GPU (`CV2Cyclone`) is 80 bytes: row e holds the flow clock, window and velocity.
Perf (RTX 3070 Ti, 1920x1080, A B interleaved in one app alone on the GPU): cloud march +4 ms over the eye from 46 km,
+3-10 ms over the CDO from 61 km, +1 ms with no cyclone in view; 128 registers. "Hurricane rotation" 0 costs nothing.

Round 2: the march evaluates the displacement once per main sample and its light march reuses it; with no cyclone in
the view's cap the march does one uniform test per sample; in time-lapse past ~2 deg of turn a frame at the RMW the
rotation fades out (the storm lock stays), so nothing strobes.

## History
The first cut held the displacement as a global across the layers with the loop inlined at five read sites: 255
registers, +13-23 ms. At 0.75 turn per window and a 0.2 blend, the dithered phases were a double exposure smeared
along arcs over a sector of the CDO's top in every settled view. Two "arms teleporting" causes found on the way were not the rotation: the arms' outline texture
read at the map direction (the storm moved through it at ~25 m/s), and the auto arm count stepping with the current wind.
A general flow of all cloud noise on the wind field (weather-v2's) was not shipped: a per-sample wind in the march and a
per-pixel one in the resolve, and the lattice seams it showed from orbit.
