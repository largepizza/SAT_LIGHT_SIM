## Now
A step of the sim clock is told apart from time running. The cloud density clipmap (the occupancy cells the march skips
empty air with) stamps each cell with a 4-bit sim-time quantum (half a cell of 40 m/s wind slide: 1.25 s at level 0,
doubling per level), so a step that is a whole multiple of 16 quanta (every 20 s of sim time at level 0, 40 s at level 1)
matched the stamps of cells baked before it. Any step of 10 s or more (8 level-0 quanta) now starts a new clipmap epoch;
steps in a burst (a scrub) switch the clipmap off until 64 frames after the last one, then one more epoch, so the 3-bit
epoch never wraps onto cells baked a few steps back. Time warp keeps the clipmap only up to 4x, where a frame's step is
far below 10 s.
The screen history of the clouds is also dropped on a clock step of more than `clouds_v2.history_time_jump_s` (30 s, no
UI; 0 = only the 600-s rule) beyond what the time warp ran this frame (the sim's own advance, dt x time scale, is
accumulated in `cv2SimAdvanceAcc`; a hitch under warp is not a jump). The Sun shadow volume already restarts on any step
over 1 s (`sun_volume_max_step_s`).

## History
Until 2026-11-10 only a step of 600 s or more counted as a jump (a new epoch, a dropped history). Steps under that (a
report's or bookmark's time, a time set, a trail scrub) that happened to be a multiple of 20 s kept stale empty clipmap
cells: cloud missing in square cell blocks over the anvil, which with time paused never expired (issue_20261107_163510,
"Cloud trails": at its shot 4, `time add 320` drew blocks in every frame and 200 frames later; `time add 330` none).
