## Now
The compiled-in cloud defaults (member initialisers in `SatelliteSim.h`) are the author's tuned look of 2026-11-10,
rounded to 2 significant figures: ~110 `clouds_v2.*` / `clouds.*` / `photometry.*` values plus the sky TAA weights
(`display.sky_taa_weight` 0.42, `display.sky_taa_weight_moving` 0.49). "Multi-scatter side escape"
(`clouds_v2.ms_side_escape`) defaults to **0.31**: the diffuse field's e^(-k t) loss models the light that leaves a
finite, lobed cloud through its sides and valleys; that light never reaches the base, so bases darken while sunlit
tops barely change. Preset-controlled fields (march steps, light steps, step growth, view / light samples, the cloud and
terrain fade distances) keep the High row; the user runs Ultra, whose row already equals their values.
Other notable moves: coverage 0.42, cover_full 0.45, density 1.6, detail 0.18, ms_octaves 3.9, ms_diffuse_gain 2.1,
ms_diffusion_g 0.52, phase_g 0.39, tonemap contrast 1.5 / range 0.95 / saturation 1.4, anvil 0.35 with thickness
1.1 km, storm_frequency 0.18, storm_scale 30, weather warp 100 km.
Six coordinate scales are baked exactly, not to 2 significant figures: the noise periods (`shape_period_m` 3099.5476,
`detail_period_m` 341.17648, `cirrus_period_m` 23438.914), the flow field's `flow_period_km` 6693.213, the mid layer's
`mid_field_km` 2470.5884 and `clouds.drift_rate` 3.7104073e-6. Each multiplies a world coordinate thousands of times its
own size (or, for the drift, months of sim time), so rounding one re-rolls every cloud: with them rounded the built-in
defaults and the user's file drew different skies at the same pose (6-14% mean difference); exact, 0.4-0.7%.
3.7e-6 vs 3.7104e-6 alone moves the map ~8 deg by late 2036.

## History
Side escape was a fixed 0.05 until 2026-11-04, then a setting defaulting to 0.11 (fitted to lobed clouds lit from the
side with `tools/cloud_micro_reference.py --contrast`). The user raised it to 0.31 as the fix for bases that read too
light from the ground (issue 20261104_180745); this bake makes it the default.
