## Now
"Anvil formation time" is how long a storm's anvil takes to spread (its radius matures over it; its density still comes up
while the towers feed it) and "Anvil decay time" is how long it lingers after the last tower: x1 for an air-mass cell, x1.3
for a multicell, x1.7 for a supercell, x0.75..1.25 per storm. Squall lines and MCSs scale their own hours-long shield rise and
linger by the same sliders (x formation / 60 min, x decay / 70 min). Towers' flared heads are joined by a smooth union, so two
anvils at one height merge instead of meeting in a seam; a head flares over a band 1.5 x "Anvil thickness" + "Anvil hang" under
its top. Seen from inside a cloud, the march treats the cloud as continuing behind the eye (no face leak) and the Sun's path
through the sample's own cloud as diffuse transmission, so a storm's interior is grey, not navy.
## History
Until 2026-11-10 the decay slider lingered anvils 1-2x of slider x 35/45/60 / 35 min (up to ~3.4x its value), formation past
~40 min changed nothing, lines ignored both, the heads' union was a hard max (a seam), and the inside of a storm took sky
light only (deep navy).
