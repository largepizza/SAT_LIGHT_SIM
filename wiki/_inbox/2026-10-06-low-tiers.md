## Now
Planetarium (the SKY_LITE sky variant) is the flat textured Earth of v1.1: the terrain march, close-up materials,
the sea's sky-reflection march and shore, beam ground spots, the procedural city (layout, glitter, roads, farms,
beaches, roof PV, solar parks) and the satellite meshes / environment probes are compiled out; cities are the
night map plus the tiled city detail textures. Potato's sky is the Sept-2026 minimal shader (DEM-only eye height).
With the volumetric clouds knocked out (both tiers) nothing of clouds v2 runs: no lightning, rain particles,
thunder or rain sound; Potato also skips the half-res composite pass. A first run picks its preset from the GPU
(`SatelliteSim::recommendedPresetForDevice`): Potato for MoltenVK on a non-Apple GPU (e.g. the 2015 MacBook Pro's
R9 M370X), Planetarium for integrated / CPU / virtual GPUs and discrete ones with <= ~2 GB VRAM, else Medium.
After an unclean exit the preset steps down one tier (full -> Planetarium -> Potato). The build fails if
`sat_sky_lite.frag.spv` passes 225 KB or `sat_sky_minimal.frag.spv` 46 KB (trimmed: 199 / 41 KB). The device-limit
check now covers per-stage storage images (the app uses 4, the guaranteed minimum). During a solar eclipse the sky
pass's exposure adapts to the eclipse sky light exactly as the probes, bloom and post-tonemap terms do.

## History
SKY_LITE had grown 142 KB (v1.1.0) -> 567 KB of SPIR-V because new features were gated by runtime knockouts or
`if (false)`, which still compile every call; the 2026-10-04 decision to draw the procedural city on Planetarium
was reverted for that reason. Crash recovery always forced Planetarium. During totality the sky pass used the day
exposure while every C++ consumer of skyExposure() used the eclipse-adapted one. Harness scripts:
`low_tiers.satcmd`, `eclipse_exposure.satcmd`.
