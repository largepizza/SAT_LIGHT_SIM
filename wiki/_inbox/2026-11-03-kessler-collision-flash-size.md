## Now
A pair collision's flash up close is white-hot and brief: light curve 0.8 e^(-t/0.08 s) + 0.2 e^(-t/0.3 s), colour
white cooling to orange with e^(-t/0.2 s). The emissive fireball still expands at 0.15 of the closing speed, but its
surface brightness is capped at its hot size (its size at 30 ms) and falls as (hot size / size)^2 as the vapour grows,
so a camera ~1 km off is no longer inside a glowing cloud half a second later. The impact frames at any time warp are
unchanged (flare ~360 at the crossing); from 250 km and from the ground the flash is the same point and glare.
