## Now
The first frame after the loading screen does no one-off work (under ~30 ms): the launch schedule's first batches,
the icon atlas, two lazily made compute pipelines, the deployment era's roster upload and a swapchain rebuild for a
saved frame-rate cap are done during the load, and a rebuild is requested only when the present mode changes. The
satellite model bakes run in parallel at load (launch to first frame ~9 s on an RTX 3070 Ti, warm caches). Ambience
voices are made on worker threads (silent until ready). At fast time rates the Kessler mirror takes all of the
worker's output within 3 ms a frame (roster changes, events and small-fragment streams included), its event log never
reallocates (a segmented vector), the reuse-history prune runs ~1 ms a frame, and the GPU debris pool grows without
waiting for the GPU (next buffers allocated ahead on a worker, old contents copied on the GPU, old buffers freed after
the frame). Launch batch builds stop at ~8 ms of wall time a frame. The harness `hitches` statistics are net of the
harness's own work (`harnessTick`); the frame profile reports `app.other` (unscoped time) and `fprof::mark` splits.
`python tools/harness/hitch_check.py` runs the time-scale ladder in four scenarios (load, a user's settings, 2031,
after the CME) and fails if a rate's p99 / max, the first frame or the boot exceed `hitch_thresholds.json`; it waits to
run alone on the GPU and reports CONTENDED (exit 3) otherwise.

## History
2026-10-21 (hitch pass 2). The first frame was ~360 ms: ~215 ms of it a swapchain rebuild requested on every launch
(applyFpsCapMode), ~50 ms ambience voices decoded on the main thread, ~25 ms launch batch builds, the rest icons,
pipelines, a joystick query and fsynced log lines. Boot was ~15 s (model bakes ~10 s serial). At 1 week/s after the
CME a frame of 400 expensive batch builds took 300-600 ms (the count cap follows the previous frame's cost); the
Kessler sync's roster / stream tail 6-14 ms, the prune 16-105 ms, each pool growth 20-30 ms. See the hitch pass 2
report for the ladder tables.
