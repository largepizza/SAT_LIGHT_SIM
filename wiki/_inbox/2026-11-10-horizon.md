## Now
The sky's limb volume (`clouds_v2.sky_ap_limb` 1) never blends rows from the two sides of the Earth's limb. A pixel
decides its side by its own ray against the sea-level sphere; a sky pixel extrapolates the first two sky rows over the gap
to the limb, a ground pixel holds the last ground row. The limb's sine comes from the eye's height above the sphere
(exact in float), not from 1 - (R / r)^2, which is ~3% off near the ground (half a row at 10 m). No new setting; same cost.
