## Now
- Graphics presets own the time-lapse lighting cut-offs (issue_20261110_190018). The light shafts through a cloud deck's
  gaps come from the light march reading the occupancy clipmap's light; the clipmap switches off past
  `clouds_v2.cdc_max_rate` x real time and the Sun transmittance volume past `clouds_v2.sun_volume_max_step_s` of sim
  time per frame. Ultra sets both to 1e6 (no cut-off: the shafts, the clouds' shadow on the sea and the air's god rays
  stay at any time rate); every other tier 4 / 1 (High = the compiled defaults). Cost at Ultra in time-lapse: see
  docs/subsystems/clouds.md "Ultra keeps the light shafts in time-lapse".
- White balance (issue_20261109_185137): the divisor (the reference sunlight's colour over its luminance) is limited to
  0.4 .. 2.5 by scaling every channel's log by one factor (`cv2WbLimit`), not by clamping each channel. Clamped per
  channel, a Sun 2-3 deg up lost its blue correction while red and green kept theirs, and cloud / ambient light read
  green-yellow (deck CIELAB a -4.4 -> +4.8 on the report's view). Full sky shader and the rain particles; SKY_LITE keeps
  the clamp.
