# Tornadoes pick a place with a storm base over it; the radar marks them

Date: 2026-11-08 (branch qa/20261108/tornado, from weather-salvage; issue 20261108_173628). Pages: weather (tornadoes),
Weather window (radar).

- What changed: a supercell's tornado has two places on the rear flank of its dominant tower (-0.65 x the shear + 0.7
  across it): OUTER, 1.1 x the tower's mature radius (the rain-free base past the rain core, when the map's low deck
  reaches there), and INNER, 0.8 x (under the tower's own base, just inside its edge, where the rain core has faded). The
  GPU base probe checks both; the funnel is drawn at the outer place while a storm base stands over it, else at the
  inner one, and keeps its place while that base holds.
- Why: the outer place alone was past the drawn tower's base. Over clear air the "a tornado needs a storm base over it"
  gate (2026-10-27) hid the funnel while the Weather window listed it on the ground. The inner place alone fixed that
  storm but buried another's funnel in rain (off Brisbane, 2036-11-25), where the outer place had a base and a clear funnel.
- Measured at the report's storm (Rc 21 km, the reporter's 20-km "Cb tower radius"): no base at 1.1, a base 2.65 km up
  at 0.9, 1.0-1.15 km up at 0.75 and inside; the funnel shows at 0.8 and is veiled by the rain core at 0.7 and inside.
- The Weather window's radar marks each tornado on the ground with a red down-pointing triangle (white outline) at the
  place drawn (waterspouts only over water). Harness: `weather radar` -> `radar.tornadoes_on_map`, `radar.tornado_marks`;
  `state` -> `weather.tornado_list[].placement / found / found_outer / found_inner / tower_km / rc_km`.
- Cost: no shader change; the second probe column is ~40 field evaluations on an idle lightning-pass thread; cloud
  march within noise with a funnel in or out of frame (contended A/B).
