## Now
Reflected clouds (rendering/sea.md, "Reflected clouds"): the hit test along the reflected ray is soft. At each of the
five altitude samples the visible cloud's distance is interpolated over the four gathered cloudTargetA texels (cloud
texels only; their bilinear share is the cover), and the sample's weight is cover x smoothstep ramps at both edges
of the -max(200 m, 2%) .. +max(2.5 km, 30%) window, each 10% of the cloud's distance wide. The strongest sample is
the hit (a weight over 0.99 stops the search), its weight is the reflected cloud's weight (hitK), and the one
bisection blends its two candidate points by a soft (25%-wide) version of its test instead of picking one. No measurable cost.

## History
2026-11-12 to 2026-11-14 the hit was yes/no on the nearest texel. Near the horizon a sample reaches its altitude
~a/angle away, so its distance changes ~10% per sea row and the window was a band a few rows deep; its edges
followed the half-res texels' distances, magnified ~4x vertically by the grazing reflection: reflected patches with
hard, stair-stepped outlines cut off flat (issue 20261114_112348, sunset over open Pacific from 179 m). Ramps of 25%
softened them further but put more partial hits under the rough sea's per-pixel reflected directions, raising the
frame-to-frame change on the 20261112_220359 path by 20-27%; 10% adds 5-13% (run-to-run noise ~10%).
